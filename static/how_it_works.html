How Trac-I Works

Legacy Secure Email Gateways (SEGs) and static reputation blocklists lag 24 to 72 hours on zero-day domains. Here is how our multi-dimensional AI architecture closes that window.

1

Multi-Feature Header Authentication

We parse raw RFC 822 / EML headers to evaluate Sender Policy Framework (SPF), DKIM cryptographic signatures, and DMARC domain alignment.

  • Display Name Spoofing: Detects adversaries pretending to be "Microsoft Security Team" or "HR Directorate" while the true SMTP sender is an external freemail domain.
  • Sender Alignment: Identifies discrepancies between the From header, Return-Path envelope sender, and deceptive Reply-To drop boxes.
  • Relay Hop Latency: Analyzes the full Received chain to detect suspicious offshore proxies and high-risk relays.
2

Lexical URL & Homoglyph Analyzer

Adversaries register Internationalized Domain Names (IDN) with Cyrillic and Greek characters that look identical to Latin letters.

  • Homoglyph & Punycode Scanner: Translates xn-- punycode and flags confusable characters like Cyrillic о (U+043E) masquerading as Latin o.
  • Brand Typosquatting: Calculates normalized Levenshtein distance against defense contractor enterprise brands and SaaS targets.
  • Shannon Entropy: Measures randomness in domain names and URL paths to identify algorithmically generated domains (DGA).
  • Deceptive Anchor Matching: Exposes links where the visible text says login.microsoftonline.com but the actual href routes to an attacker's server.
3

NLP Urgency & Psychological Coercion Engine

Spear-phishing relies heavily on psychological pressure, false authority, and manufactured deadlines.

  • Urgency Levers: Identifies strict deadlines ("within 24 hours", "immediate response required", "final notice").
  • Fear of Consequences: Flags threats of account suspension, security clearance revocation, or disciplinary action.
  • Credential Solicitation: Detects requests to "verify password", "re-authenticate CAC/MFA", or "confirm identity".
  • Anti-Filter DOM Inspection: Uncovers hidden zero-font text (font-size: 0px) used by phishers to trick legacy spam filters.
4

Machine Learning Threat Scoring & Explainable AI (XAI)

All extracted vectors are synthesized into a calibrated ensemble of Random Forest and Gradient Boosted Trees.

  • Precise Risk Scoring: Yields a calibrated 0-100% threat probability with dual-model confidence meters.
  • Explainable Attribution: Breaks down the mathematical percentage points each risk factor contributed to the overall score.
  • Safe Virtual Sandbox: Renders visual landing page previews in an isolated DOM container without script execution.
