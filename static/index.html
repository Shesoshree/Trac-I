Spam versus Scams - How Are They Different?

Understanding the difference between spam and scams is crucial for your safety. While spam filters block annoying junk advertisements, Trac-I specializes in detecting weaponized attacks designed to steal your credentials and data.

Intent & Severity

Spam is annoying Unwanted promotional messages that clutter your inbox but generally do not pose active cybersecurity threats.
Scams are malicious Deliberately weaponized to steal credentials, execute wire fraud, harvest enterprise SSO tokens, or deploy ransomware.

Purpose & Goal

Spam advertises Commercial sellers attempting to promote products, discount coupons, or newsletter subscriptions.
Scams steal & compromise Adversaries impersonating IT Directors, HR executives, or Microsoft 365 to harvest passwords and corporate intelligence.

Transparency & Deception

Spam is clear what it is Promotional layout with standard sender details and functional unsubscribe links.
Scams disguise themselves Uses deceptive Cyrillic homoglyphs, newly registered domains with valid SSL, zero-font hidden text, and urgency coercion.
